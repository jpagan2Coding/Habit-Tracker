Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you begin.

Is Habit Tracker free?

Yes. You can create and track your habits from the Dashboard at no cost.

Can I track multiple habits?

Yes. Add as many habits as you need and review them together in your progress view.

How are streaks calculated?

A streak counts consecutive days where you complete a habit.

Can I edit or delete habits?

Habit editing and deletion are planned for a future sprint.

Is my data saved securely?

Your habits are stored in the application database and are only used to provide your tracker experience.
